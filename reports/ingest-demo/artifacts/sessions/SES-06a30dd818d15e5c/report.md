# Speaking report — 1 March
During an unscripted conversation about planning a weekend trip, the learner asked a friend: "I'm with a doubt about the booking." They clarified that they wanted to ask whether breakfast was included, rather than express distrust of the hotel. No target expression had been supplied.
Later, while spontaneously suggesting that the team arrange a discussion, the learner said: "We should make a meeting on Friday." They meant schedule a discussion, not attend one or create a physical object. No wording was supplied by the teacher.
The learner also said "I'd like a coffee" when ordering politely in a café. This was appropriate for the situation. Punctuation and capitalization in this report are editorial.
