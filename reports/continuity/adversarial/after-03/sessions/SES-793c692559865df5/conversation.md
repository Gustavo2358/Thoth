# Conversation — support, recast and an opportunity

## User
Our team could not attend the planning meeting. If I know earlier, I would change the plan.

## Assistant
So if you'd known earlier, you would have changed the plan. What stopped you from finding out?

## User
Why did you change it? Can you explain briefly?

## Assistant
You are imagining a different past. Both the condition and its possible result need to refer to that unreal past, rather than an open possibility now.

## User
I understand the contrast, but let's move on to the actual release.

## Assistant
Describe the tension between the deadline and your decision. You can start with "Even though...", then supply your own reason.

## User
Even though the deadline was close, we delayed the release because the checks failed.

## Assistant
Now tell me about your new chair. When referring to expense, put the expensive adjective on the item rather than on the price. Describe your purchase without copying a sentence.

## User
My new chair was incredibly expensive, but it made long meetings more comfortable.

## Assistant
How would things have been different if your team had learned earlier? Answer whichever way you find natural.

## User
We were too late. Anyway, the new rollout worked.

## Assistant
Here are fifteen synonyms you could memorize for that discussion.

## User
Please don't give me long lists of synonyms; one useful contrast is enough for our conversations.
