# Review the next teacher prompt

Use a fresh teacher's perspective. Assess the actual Markdown with its audit and source conversations available to the reviewer. The teacher receives only the Markdown. Record `meets`, `partial`, or `fails`, a concrete reason, and the prompt SHA-256. These are subjective judgments, never probabilities or a mastery score. A model can assist review; a human should check disputed claims.

| Dimension | Question | A failure looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Continuity | Can a fresh teacher continue useful previous work and respect durable requests? | Learner must restate teaching preferences or important current work. |
| Evidence fidelity | Is each personal instruction and learner claim traceable to the audit and source? | Teacher-only claims, psychological labels, local praise promoted as permanent preference. |
| Relevance | Is selection useful for the next conversation? | Historical dump, many targets, old difficulty taught forever. |
| Pedagogical strategy | Do practice, observe and transfer lead to different actions? | Same correction policy applied to every state; feedback ends without new production. |
| Non-priming | Are observation and recovery protected from answer leakage? | Target phrase/examples supplied before a diagnostic attempt. |
| Conversational freedom | Can the teacher follow meaning and productive tangents? | Scripted questions, expected answers, forced expression or completion checklist. |
| Compactness | Is the prompt small and coherent? | Repeated history or an instruction cut mid-sentence. |
| Goal alignment | Does the conversation serve explicit goals even when strongest errors concern another topic? | Error agenda replaces the learner's stated purpose. |

Check six histories: recurring difficulty → practice; recent clean unassisted success → transfer; singleton → optional observation; explicit durable preference → instruction; one successful intervention → evidence only; explicit goal unrelated to strongest difficulty → meaningful goal-led topic with selective practice.

The controlled campaign checks selected properties using separate gold. This rubric adds prose review; neither substitutes for observing the external teacher in a real spoken session. Benchmark authors know the fixtures and must disclose that bias.
